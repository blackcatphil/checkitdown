# KnockMark

Two knuckles on the table — a check, in the poker sense. Not a checkmark tick, not a
card suit, no chips, no dice.

Two overlapping filled ellipses (the knuckles, `#8A66C9`) with a broken ring around the
leading one (`#A98CE8`). The ring is a knock spreading, and it is the i-dot in the
wordmark lockup.

**Cadence: once on load, replayed on hover.** Not a loop — a permanently ticking logo
competes with live data for the same attention. Two rings, second entering at 17% of a
3.4s cycle, so it reads as knock-knock rather than one pulse.

**At rest a static ring holds at 0.62 opacity.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the
animation is frozen with that ring visible — never a bare pair of dots.

Works at 16px: the ring survives, the second knuckle merges. Single colour on paper.
